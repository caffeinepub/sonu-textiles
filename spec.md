# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Build a bilingual (Hindi/English) business website for Sonu Textiles covering clothing products, mehndi services, about us, and contact — with a warm, festive Indian aesthetic.

**Planned changes:**
- Homepage with hero banner, business name "Sonu Textiles / सोनू टेक्सटाइल्स", tagline in both languages, and navigation links to all sections
- Language toggle button in navbar to switch all UI text between Hindi and English (session-persistent)
- Clothing section: responsive product gallery with name (Hindi + English), description, price, and "Order Now" button opening a modal order form (collects customer name, phone, address, product); orders stored in backend
- Mehndi section: design image gallery (4 images), pricing/rates table in both languages, appointment booking form (name, phone, date, time, service type) stored in backend with confirmation message
- About Us section: business description in both Hindi and English mentioning clothing and mehndi services
- Contact Us section: form (name, phone, email, message) stored in backend with success message; business phone and address placeholder displayed
- Warm, festive visual theme using deep red, gold, and ivory with elegant typography suitable for a traditional Indian business

**User-visible outcome:** Visitors can browse Sonu Textiles' clothing and mehndi offerings, place orders, book mehndi appointments, and send enquiries — all in either Hindi or English.
